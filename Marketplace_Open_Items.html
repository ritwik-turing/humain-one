How to read this. V1 is a clickable mock: it makes a working assumption at every fork so the journey holds together for a demo. This register lists every one of those forks that is actually an open decision, not a settled design. Payments is the obvious one, and there are 36 others. On the call I will walk the flow once, then step through the four items tagged DEMO first, since those gate what we can honestly show HUMAIN.

How to read the tags

Every row has two tags. One answers whose call is it, the other answers how urgent. They are independent — an item can be ours to build yet still urgent, or HUMAIN's call yet safe to defer.

OWNER — whose call is it?
TURINGOurs. We decide and build it, no external dependency.
HUMAINTheirs. We can't move until they answer.
JOINTNeeds both of us in the room. A meeting, not an email.
LEGALA compliance / counsel question. Not for a PM to guess.
BLOCKS — how urgent, tied to which milestone?
DEMOMust be settled before we show HUMAIN. Only 4 of the 37.
BUILDNeeded to ship, not to demo. Stays mocked for the call.
DEFERCan stay mocked now, decided later in build scoping.

Reading a row at a glance: DEMO JOINT = get HUMAIN and us in a room before the demo (payments is exactly this). BUILD TURING = our engineering backlog, not a call blocker. DEMO LEGAL = we need a legal answer before we can honestly show it (data residency).
