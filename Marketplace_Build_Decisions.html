Provisioned access via magic link + credentials DECIDED

For the initial versions we expose an agent-specific magic link plus client ID / secret for provisioned access, instead of building a full tenant agent-distribution flow (assign to users / groups, SSO). The entitle step now issues the link and credentials.

WHY
This meaningfully cuts post-acquisition complexity for launch — no org directory, group management, or SSO wiring needed to get an agent into a buyer's hands.
CONFIRM WITH HUMAIN
Confirm the approach; full tenant-wide distribution (assign to users / groups, SSO) is explicitly post-LEAP.
5

Level-2 research: acquisition metrics for the Listings card DONE · 18 Jul

Research the right acquisition and usage metrics for org acquisition vs retail acquisition, to put the correct signals on the listing card (beyond the current placeholders, which are aligned to the observability layer).

FINDINGS (verified, 24 claims)
B2B cards are metric-sparse — no major platform shows install/deployment counts on cards. What drives org acquisition: transparent pricing (72% more likely to buy), free trials (#1 influence, 77%), time-to-value (57% expect ROI in 3 months), security posture (81% weigh breach history). Card set shipped: cert-tier badge, Trust score, go-live time, trial line, transparent SAR price, residency tag. Usage stays at detail level only.
APPLIED
Listing cards rebuilt in the V1 mock accordingly; usage counts removed from cards, kept on detail pages where AWS-style evaluation evidence lives.
6

Certification metrics research (benchmarking + Guardian) DONE · 18 Jul

Define the certification metrics for the listing's Certification section through competitive benchmarking and by analysing the Guardian endpoints. Guardian documentation shared by Tirthankar.

FINDINGS
Benchmark: Microsoft's two-tier model (independently audited Certification vs self-declared Publisher Attestation, published verbatim); Salesforce publishes the security-review pass date on the listing. No incumbent publishes AI-quality numbers (accuracy, prompt-injection results, hallucination rates) — publishing measured ones from our own harness, labelled with method + version, is a differentiator. Platform reality (from the team's certification API): 5 automated checks (package structure, security scan, config, dependencies, eval validation), states pending → certifying → pending_approval → certified; Guardian auto-certs in the dev flow but is unavailable for LEAP (stubbed per 17 Jul scope).
APPLIED + CONFIRM WITH HUMAIN
Guardian integration guide (embedded_audit.v1) analysed in full. Key contract facts: Guardian returns findings (severity, confidence band, evidence, repro steps) and a qualitative verdict band (ready / needs work / hold) — by design it issues no scores, no pass/fail, no gates; the certification decision is the platform's own policy applied to Guardian evidence (reference policy: band = ready AND zero critical findings). Audit domains: security, reliability, performance, accessibility, api_contracts, functionality, code quality. Threads/cycles give re-audit history; source integrity is a server-verified SHA-256. The V1 mock's certification panel now mirrors this exactly: GUARDIAN AUDIT (band, findings, domains, digest) + MEASURED Prism numbers + PUBLISHER-ATTESTED declarations, with the platform policy stated on the panel. Still to align with HUMAIN: who operates the Guardian org/key for marketplace certification, and the exact certification policy thresholds.
